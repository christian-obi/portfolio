01 · About

Data scientist by craft.
Storyteller on stage.

I'm a data scientist and blockchain analyst specialising in turning complex on-chain data into clear, actionable intelligence. I build interactive dashboards, statistical models and AI-driven tools that decode DeFi protocols, track whale behaviour, and surface insights across ecosystems like Core, NEAR, Arbitrum and Monad.

As a speaker, I share insights on blockchain analytics and data-driven decision making at conferences and community events. Whether it's visualising on-chain metrics or speaking on stage, I bring a detail-oriented, curious approach to every project.
